in Problems 1 and 4, along with positive trends in the
remaining problems analyzed. These results suggest that
GAI can enrich Socratic dialogue by supporting concep-
tual accuracy, inferential elaboration, and metacognitive
revision. The absence of negative performance impacts,
together with the overall improvement observed, supports
the pedagogical feasibility and potential benefits of inte-
grating GAI tools within this disciplinary context.
Analysis of the secondary research questions further
revealed that GAI mediation positively influenced stu-
dents’ perceptions of learning. Significant increases were
observed in kinematics self-efficacy and intrinsic motiva-
tion among prospective teachers, who evaluated the role of
GAI in their learning process favorably. These subjective
gains were consistent with the objective improvements in
performance. Cognitive load, however, exhibited a more
complex pattern. While GAI facilitated access to informa-
tion and conceptual clarification, it also required students
to engage in monitoring and quality control of generated
responses. This finding underscores the importance of
pedagogical designs that integrate GAI as a teacher-
supervised cognitive scaffold rather than as an answer-
providing tool.
Within the conditions examined in this study, the
SOCRATIC-IA model provides a preliminary instructional
framework for integrating teacher-guided Socratic dia-
logue and generative AI in kinematics problem solving.
The findings support further investigation of its feasibility
and pedagogical value, but they do not establish its
effectiveness beyond the present sample and instructional
context. Further research using larger samples, controlled
or counterbalanced designs, equivalent nonidentical tasks,
and measures of retention and transfer is needed to
determine whether the observed patterns can be replicated
and attributed more specifically to GAI-mediated Socratic
support.
By integrating Socratic dialogue with the adaptive
and feedback capabilities of GAI, this approach not only
enhances conceptual and numerical problem solving in
translational kinematics but also fosters self-efficacy and
intrinsic motivation—key factors for autonomous and
lifelong learning. More broadly, this research opens
avenues
for
exploring
how
pedagogically
grounded
applications of generative AI can transform STEAM
learning environments by cultivating deeper, more trans-
ferable forms of reasoning.

levels
